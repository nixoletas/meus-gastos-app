Account deletion

Português

Meus Gastos (an expense tracking app) lets you delete your account and all of your data at any time.

How to delete from the app

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type delete to confirm and finish.

Cannot get into the app?

Send an email to contato@meusgastos.dev.br from your account address, with the subject "Delete my account". We will do it within 7 days.

What gets erased

Your account and all associated data: expenses, categories, subcategories and limits. Deletion is permanent and immediate; there is no retention period and the data cannot be recovered.
